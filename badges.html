Badges

Every tool in Adopt, Trial or Assess on the Adoption Radar gets a badge. Nobody buys one and nobody hands one out: the ring comes from the public rubric, and each tool links its evidence.

Made one of these tools? Put its badge in your README or on your site. It is a live image, so it shows your tool's ring today. If the data moves your tool, the badge changes on the next build. Hold is not an award, so Hold tools get no badge.
