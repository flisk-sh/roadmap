Badges

Every tool in Adopt, Trial or Assess on the Adoption Radar gets a badge. Nobody buys one and nobody hands one out: the ring comes from the public rubric, and each tool links its evidence.

Made one of these tools? Find it below and copy its badge into your README. It is a live image: if the data moves your tool, the badge changes on the next build. Hold is not an award, so Hold tools get no badge.
