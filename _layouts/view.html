How to read a tile

Top left is the element number, top right the license (OS open source, Fr free, Fm free tier, Pd paid). The big letters are the symbol, the bar at the bottom is the ring. The formula in each tooltip is its two Compass scores: Dx ergonomics and Pa parity, in tens.
